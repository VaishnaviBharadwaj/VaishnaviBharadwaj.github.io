Research &

2026 Presented · Journal selection ongoing
Conversational Custodians: Deploying LLM-Powered Agentic Systems for Dynamic Digitisation and Contextual Revival of Intangible Cultural Heritage
Venue options: Digital Scholarship in the Humanities (DSH) — Oxford University Press · SSCI-indexed or Journal of Human Values (JHV) — SAGE Publications · Scopus-indexed

Proposes CAAS — a Governance-Aware Retrieval Architecture (GARA) that embeds community-defined access constraints directly inside the retrieval layer for intangible cultural heritage (ICH). Built a 12,239-node Heritage Memory Graph fusing LLM triple extraction with expert annotation across six Malabar performing arts traditions (Theyyam, Koodiyattam, Kathakali, Padayani, Mudiyettu, Mohiniyattam). Introduces a formal governance function G(q,c) with a hard access-tier threshold and dual-stage sensitivity gating to ensure restricted sacred knowledge is structurally excluded — not statistically penalised — during LLM synthesis. Evaluated across six experiments with 94.2% HMG accuracy and 84.3% community stakeholder agreement.
