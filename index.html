01 / About

The gap between
predicting and understanding

Research interest

Trustworthy, interpretable AI for education. Can a model ground every answer in the lecture it learned from — and can we judge that independently, instead of taking fluency on faith? Neuro-symbolic reasoning asks the same question of representations themselves.

Interpretable AI AI for Education Neuro-Symbolic Reasoning Grounding & Faithfulness RAG Evaluation Research Track
"The system screams a broken note, but hides the song the logic wrote."

I'm a software and infrastructure engineer with 3.5+ years at Accenture, where I've worked across telecommunications (AT&T) and insurance (Allianz). My work spans Linux server reliability, API observability, backend development, and production support.

I hold a B.Tech in Applied Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering from the Haldia Institute of Technology (CGPA: 9.14). Building on that foundation, I am currently completing an MBA in Business Analytics at Manipal Academy of Higher Education (CGPA 9.45), and independently built a full-stack educational platform — the Data Science Learning Hub — covering 12 subjects, 70+ modules, and the complete GATE DA syllabus from scratch.

My interest in this traces back to Class 10, 2014, watching Cosmos: A Spacetime Odyssey — Neil deGrasse Tyson described how humans once connected scattered stars into constellations: patterns that live in our interpretation, not between the stars. How do you tell a real pattern from one you merely projected? A decade later, my churn project put that question on production footing. 87.73% accuracy was the headline, but the part I cared about was the coefficient analysis underneath — which features the model actually leaned on, and what each one was worth in dollars. A high score is easy to be impressed by; understanding has to be checked. That conviction — that a model should show its work before we trust its answer — is what points me toward interpretable AI for education.

Proposes CAAS — a Governance-Aware Retrieval Architecture (GARA) that embeds community-defined access constraints directly inside the retrieval layer for intangible cultural heritage (ICH). Built a 12,239-node Heritage Memory Graph fusing LLM triple extraction with expert annotation across six Malabar performing arts traditions (Theyyam, Koodiyattam, Kathakali, Padayani, Mudiyettu, Mohiniyattam). Introduces a formal governance function G(q,c) with a hard access-tier threshold and dual-stage sensitivity gating to ensure restricted sacred knowledge is structurally excluded — not statistically penalised — during LLM synthesis.
